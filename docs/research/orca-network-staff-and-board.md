## Who handles sightings

**The Whale Sighting Network (WSN) is the part of Orca Network that takes in, checks and relays sightings**, so its coordinators and assistants are the likeliest people to send the Community Group's day posts to SalishSea.io; who sends is for Orca Network to decide. Their names already appear in the group's posts and in Orca Network's published reports: a day post of 12 November thanks "ON staff member Rachel Haight for relaying this report", and Justine Buckmaster is credited on the Lime Kiln hydrophone reports of 14 November.

**Orca Network has had joint leadership since 1 July 2026:** Stephanie Raymond leads programs and Joy Brungardt leads operations, as co-Executive Directors. Susan Berta, who began the volunteer whale-watching work that became Orca Network, is Founding Director, and Howard Garrett is President of the board.
